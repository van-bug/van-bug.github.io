# About Us

VanBUG (Vancouver Bioinformatics User Group) is a non-profit association of researchers, other professionals and students in the B.C. Lower Mainland who have an interest in the field of bioinformatics, computational biology, and data sciences. VanBUG meets every month from September through April. Research presentations by bioinformatics leaders, students and industry representatives are followed by networking over pizza and refreshments.

Visit our sister groups for bioinformatics events in Montreal ([MonBUG](https://www.monbug.ca/)), Toronto ([TorBUG](https://torbug.org/)), Edmonton ([yegBUG](https://yegbug.ca/)) and Ottawa ([OttBUG](https://ottbug.ca/))!
